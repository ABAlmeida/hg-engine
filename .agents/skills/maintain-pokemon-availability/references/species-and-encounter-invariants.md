# Species and Encounter Invariants

Read this reference only when its subject is relevant to the requested change.

- Establish later-generation implementation readiness from current repository
  source. Do not infer completeness from an upstream status statement or from
  the presence of a species constant.
- Compiling a new species does not place it in encounters, trainers, gifts, or
  other obtainable content.
- Internal species IDs after Arceus are not National Pokédex numbers. IDs
  494–543 are Egg, Bad Egg, and numbered placeholders; Victini is National Dex
  494 but engine species ID 544.
- Wild double battles remain disabled by the current project design. Treat
  `documentation/CAPTURE_RULES_PLAN.md` as authoritative if encounter-mode
  behavior is intentionally reconsidered.
