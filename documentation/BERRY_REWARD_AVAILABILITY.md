# Berry Reward Availability

Last updated: 2026-08-14

This document tracks Berries as candidates for first-victory trainer rewards.
The candidate set is the 67 standard Berry items recognized by `IS_ITEM_BERRY`
and stocked by the Goldenrod Berry Shop. Inclusion does not imply that every
Berry currently has a held effect.

All 67 candidates are already obtainable through that shop. A trainer reward
therefore adds an earlier or free finite source rather than introducing an
otherwise unavailable item. Berries outside the standard set, such as Hyper
Berries, are excluded until they are deliberately added to normal progression.

## Status summary

| Status | Count |
|---|---:|
| Standard Berry candidates | 67 |
| Available from the Goldenrod Berry Shop | 67 |
| Assigned as trainer rewards | 5 |
| Manually verified trainer rewards | 0 |

## Updating this tracker

When adding a Berry to `data/trainer_rewards.csv`:

1. Change **Trainer reward** from `Unassigned` to the trainer name, numeric
   trainer ID, location, and quantity.
2. Leave **Verification** as `Not verified` until the reward has been received
   successfully in a current build.
3. After that manual check, change **Verification** to `Verified` and update
   the summary counts above.
4. Do not change **Other normal source** unless the Berry's non-trainer
   availability has also changed.
